## 📌 Overview

**Hangout Dinajpur** is a web application designed to help locals and travelers discover the best hangout spots, local food destinations, and historical attractions across Dinajpur. Whether you're looking for a relaxing evening at Ramsagar, exploring the heritage of Kantaji Temple, or finding cozy local cafes, Hangout Dinajpur helps you plan your next experience effortlessly.

---

## ✨ Features

- 📍 **Explore Local Spots:** Curated recommendations for popular cafes, restaurants, parks, and hidden gems in Dinajpur.
- 🏛️ **Historical Landmarks:** Detailed information and quick guides for iconic spots like Kantaji Temple, Ramsagar National Park, and Dinajpur Rajbari.
- 🗺️ **Location-Based Discovery:** Easily browse and filter spots by category or location.
- 🎯 **Smart Recommendations:** Suggestions tailored to your mood, group size, and preferences.
- 📱 **Fully Responsive UI:** Smooth user interface optimized for desktop, tablet, and mobile browsers.
